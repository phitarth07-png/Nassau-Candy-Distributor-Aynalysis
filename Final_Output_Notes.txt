PROJECT INTRODUCTION

This project focuses on analyzing the sales performance of Nassau Candy Distributor using Python
 and data analysis techniques. The dataset contains product-level business information such as Sales,
 Cost, and Gross Profit. The project analyzes this data to identify top-performing products, compare sales
 and profit, analyze costs, and determine products with better profit margins.


PROJECT OBJECTIVES

1. To analyze the sales performance of different products.
2. To identify products with the highest sales and gross profit.
3. To analyze product costs and compare them with sales.
4. To identify products with the best profit margins.
5. To visualize important business information using graphs.
6. To generate useful insights for better business decision-making.


PROJECT OVERVIEW

This project analyzes the business performance of Nassau Candy Distributor
